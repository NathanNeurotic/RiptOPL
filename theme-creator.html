Theme Creator

Make a RiptOPL theme in your browser. Move things around on the live preview, add your own pictures and fonts, and download a theme folder that is ready to copy to your PS2. Everything stays on your computer: nothing you add is uploaded anywhere.

  1. Pick a start on the right: RiptOPL's own theme or a ready layout.
  2. Click anything on the preview to move it, resize it or change it. Pick a screen above the preview to edit that screen.
  3. Download the theme and copy it to your PS2.

In widescreen RiptOPL draws pictures and text 3/4 as wide, but leaves their positions where they were, so edges no longer sit on the grid. This is what the PS2 does too. squeezes the positions as well, so the whole layout stays on its grid.

    More kinds

    Only needed after editing the config file by hand: RiptOPL stops reading a screen at the first missing number.

    Your pictures

    Add PNG, JPG, WebP or GIF pictures. Others are turned into PNG for you. Each one is saved as name.png in the theme folder; use that name in an element's picture field.

    Badges

    The little pictures on the game list and info page that show each game's console, disc, file format, star rating, players, video mode, screen shape and picture lines. Click a badge to use your own picture.

    RiptOPL's other pictures

    Click one to swap it for your own. Your version goes in the theme folder under the same name.

    Fonts

    Add TrueType or OpenType fonts (.ttf, .otf), then pick which font slot uses each one.

    Sounds

    RiptOPL plays these from the theme's sound folder in place of its own. Sound effects are PS2 ADPCM (.adp) files; the music is Ogg Vorbis. The preview does not play them.

    Other files

    Anything else to carry in the theme folder, such as sounds. Pick a folder inside the theme first if it needs one (for example sound).

    conf_theme.cfg

    This is the file the creator writes. You can edit it here or paste one in, then press Apply.

      Download

      Put it on your PS2

      1. Zip: unzip it. You get a folder named thm_YourName. Copy that folder into the THM folder on your memory card, USB drive, HDD or network share (the same place RiptOPL keeps its other themes).
      2. One-file theme: copy thm_YourName.tar straight into that THM folder. No unzipping.
      3. In RiptOPL open Settings → Theme Settings, pick it under Theme, then save.
